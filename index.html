Country-aware, not symbol-swapped

A US estimate is different from a UK estimate.

Estiquote changes the currency, planning value and construction units for your selected country. US projects use square feet and US material quantities; the UK and most other markets use metric units. Bundled World Bank economy-wide purchasing-power data translates the UK guide baseline and works offline.

United States · USD United Kingdom · GBP Australia · AUD Canada · CAD India · INR Japan · JPY
Clear about the limits.

Purchasing-power data is not a live construction index or supplier feed. Unit conversions preserve equivalent quantities, but local pack sizes and trade conventions can vary. Actual prices vary by city, access, taxes, specification and contractor.
