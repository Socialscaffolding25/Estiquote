Privacy

Last updated 17 August 2026

The short version

Estiquote does not require an account in this version. Your saved estimates, written quote entries, trade markups, country preference and optional Trade business logo are stored locally on your iPhone. We do not include third-party advertising or behavioural analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app stores project choices, estimate results, contractor names you enter, written quote totals, trade markups, your selected country and an optional business logo using Apple’s local app storage. The system photo picker only provides the image you choose. This information remains on the device unless you choose to share a generated report, copy or device backup.

Country market data

Country pricing factors are bundled with Estiquote and work offline. The app does not send your selected country to the World Bank or another market-data provider.

Apple purchases

Apple processes Project Pass and Trade payments. Estiquote receives verified product and entitlement information needed to unlock access, but does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple’s privacy policy and App Store terms apply to those transactions.

Sharing and PDF reports

When you tap Share, Estiquote creates a temporary PDF on the device and presents Apple’s share sheet. You decide which person or service receives it. A Trade client report can contain your project details, guide values, indicative client total and optional business logo, but it excludes your internal markup percentage, contractor names and saved comparison quotes.

Deleting information

Delete a saved project in the app to remove that project, its quote entries and its saved markup. Remove the optional business logo from Client PDF branding. Deleting the app removes Estiquote’s local app data from the device, subject to any device backups or reports you have already shared.

Privacy questions

Email support@estiquote.app with “Privacy” in the subject line.
