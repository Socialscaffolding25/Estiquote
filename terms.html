Planning guidance, not a quote

Estiquote provides indicative project cost ranges and material guide values for early planning. It is not a contractor quotation, tender, survey, valuation, engineering assessment, tax calculation or professional advice. Do not treat a guide value as a guaranteed price.

Your responsibility

Actual costs vary with scope, access, location, specification, site conditions, taxes, permits, timing and contractor rates. Before making a commitment, confirm the scope with appropriately qualified professionals, check local requirements and obtain at least three detailed written quotes where practical.

Country and material models

Country conversion uses bundled World Bank economy-wide purchasing-power data applied to Estiquote’s UK guide baseline, with bundled local-currency fallbacks where purchasing-power data is unavailable. Estiquote also displays the construction measurement system assigned to the selected market and converts equivalent material quantities where supported. It is not a construction-specific index or live supplier feed, and it does not guarantee every retailer’s pack size or local trade convention.

Free, Project Pass and Trade

  • Free provides one saved top-line estimate.
  • Project Pass is a one-time Apple in-app purchase that unlocks the full tools for one active homeowner project at a time. Replacing the active project removes that project and its saved quote comparison and markup from the iPhone after confirmation.
  • Trade is a monthly Apple subscription with unlimited saved projects, markup, quote comparison and client PDF tools, including an optional business logo stored on the iPhone.

The app displays the current price and billing terms for your App Store country before purchase.
