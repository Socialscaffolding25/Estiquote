Support

Estiquote for iPhone

Contact Estiquote

Email support@estiquote.app. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you expected to happen. Do not email sensitive payment-card details.

Restore a purchase

Open Estiquote, tap Plans or your current tier badge on Home, then choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID that made the original purchase. Project Pass and an active Trade subscription are eligible to restore.

Manage or cancel Trade

Open the membership screen and choose Manage Trade subscription, or use your iPhone’s App Store subscription settings. Apple manages Trade billing and cancellation.

Change country or currency

Open Estimate or Materials and tap the current country. Choose another country from the searchable list. Estiquote changes both screens together and shows the market model, currency and measurement units being used. US area and supported material quantities appear in US customary units; the UK and most other markets use metric units.

Replace a Project Pass project

Project Pass supports one active project at a time. Generate the new estimate and choose Replace active project when prompted. Estiquote warns you before removing the current active project, its saved quote comparison and markup from this iPhone. Choose Keep current project to cancel. Trade removes this project limit.

Add your logo to a Trade report

Open a saved project with Trade active, scroll to Client PDF branding and choose Add business logo. Select a PNG or JPEG from Apple’s photo picker. The logo is stored locally on the iPhone and appears in the header of client PDF reports. Use Change logo or Remove logo at any time.

Delete your data

Touch and hold a project in the Projects tab and choose Delete. Its saved quotes and markup are deleted with it. Remove a saved business logo from Client PDF branding. Estiquote does not require an account in this version.
